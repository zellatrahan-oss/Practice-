The Spring Formal

Black tie. Live band. Four hours. One rule, and the rule is load-bearing.

Admission requirement One (1) verified plus-one. Per attendee. No exceptions.

Door staff have been briefed, issued the guest manifest, and instructed not to negotiate. Solo entries will be walked to the parking lot, where the Parliamentarian will read them Article VII in full, out loud, at a reasonable pace.

Bringing your cousin? Run the eligibility checker first. We have seen things. Several of those things were cousins.

Ongoing chapter initiative

Asset Recovery: Where in the world is Harry Black?

In March 2024 our Treasurer Emeritus stepped out of a chapter meeting to “take a call.” He has not been seen since. Neither has the formal contingency fund. The Alumni Relations Committee has converted the search into a recruiting exercise, because that is the kind of upside-oriented culture we run here.

Harry Black remains at large.

Select a country to dispatch the Alumni Relations Committee.

Intelligence from the committee

  • The last three wires cleared from a country in Asia.
  • That country has a population above one billion.
  • His most recent LinkedIn post was captioned “emerging markets, emerging me.” It had 412 likes.
  • Photo evidence shows ancient temples, excellent street food, and our fog machine.

Harry, if you are reading this: nobody is angry. We would simply like the $4,200 and the fog machine. The fog machine was rented in the Treasurer's name, which as of March 2024 is also your name.
